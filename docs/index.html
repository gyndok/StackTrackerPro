What's Inside

Everything a serious player needs

🃏

Full Hand Logger

Capture a hand in seconds at the table — tap the card button, pick your hole cards, done. Enrich it later with tap-only entry: live pot tracking, auto-advancing streets, villain hands, and automatic winner detection at showdown.

🎙️

Voice Hand Notes

Dictate what you remember about a hand and the transcript attaches to it — on screen as your reference while you enter the details, and in your session recap. Transcription happens entirely on your device.

💥

Big-Pot Detection & Break Debrief

Report a stack change after a big pot and the app offers to log the hand. At breaks, it reviews your swings and asks about anything unexplained — so your session story stays complete.

📤

Share Hand Histories

Text any logged hand as a clean, formatted hand history with suit symbols — straight from the hand or right after saving it. Perfect for group chats and study partners.

📊

Live Stack Tracking

Track your stack in real time with a live graph, M-ratio, and big blind count. Color-coded zones give instant feedback on your stack health with actionable coaching tips.

🤖

AI-Powered Updates

Just type "I have 32k" or "310 left" — the app extracts stack counts, blinds, field sizes, bounties, and rebuys automatically. Works with Apple Intelligence on supported devices.

📷

Scan Blind Structures

Snap a photo of any tournament poster or blind schedule and OCR extracts the levels automatically. No manual entry — just point, shoot, and play.

🎰

Tournaments & Cash Games

Full support for both formats. Track buy-ins, rebuys, bounties, add-ons, and payouts for tournaments. Track stakes, hourly rates, and cash-outs for cash sessions.

📈

Deep Analytics

Cumulative profit charts, ROI, win rate, hourly rate, performance by venue and game type, ITM rate, and biggest wins — all filterable and sortable.

🎬

Video Recaps & Share Cards

Generate animated recap videos with your stack graph and key stats. Create polished share cards for Stories, Feed, or X/Twitter. Celebrate every milestone.

🔍

Pre-Game Scouting Reports

Auto-generated strategy breakdowns before you sit down. Analyzes blind structure, calculates critical levels, estimates ITM spots, and recommends an approach.

☁️

iCloud Sync & Export

Your data syncs automatically across all your Apple devices. Import historical sessions via CSV. Export everything for external analysis. Your data, your way.

📝

Session Notes & Photos

Jot general session notes with stack context and blind level attached automatically. Capture timestamped photos of chip stacks and buy-in receipts. Never lose the story of a session.
